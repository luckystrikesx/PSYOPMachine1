02   How we solve it

The PSYOP playbook

Here is the exact playbook we used to help our clients grow their personal brands, become authorities in their niche, and get more leads and clients on LinkedIn and X:

  1. 01

    Your voice

    We map your voice during our onboarding interview and we extract your writing style from previous posts to establish a baseline. We also study your competitors and founders whose writing you like.

  2. 02

    Content pillars

    We listen to your ideas and conduct in-depth market research to understand what works in your niche. We come back with 3-5 content pillars and 3 post examples written in your voice within 24-48h.

  3. 03

    Written, approved and scheduled

    Posts are drafted in batches, then sent to you for approval. Once you sign them off, we schedule 4 weeks of content across LinkedIn and X. Nothing goes live without your approval.

  4. 04

    Conversion and clients

    We track what works and measure success by revenue impact. We double down on the best-performing content. The pipeline gets heavier each month.
